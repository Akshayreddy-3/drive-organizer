**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `Drive-Organizer-Mac.dmg`, open it, and drag Drive Organizer onto Applications. The first open is blocked once: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

**Windows:** download and run `Drive-Organizer-Setup.exe`. On the blue warning box, click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Nothing else to install: Python and everything the app needs are inside. Setup continues in the app's Settings page.
